## Why the Shared Construction Rule Passes

A's H rule was compared with S's already frozen edge-interpolation
construction, not accepted merely because both say "complete":

- The cross-product plane functional has the same zero set as the
  barycentric/affine functional in S. A nonzero plane normal is required
  before naming a unique plane.
- A's edge formula
  `(h(V)W-h(W)V)/(h(V)-h(W))` is algebraically identical to S's
  directed division with `t=h(V)/(h(V)-h(W))`.
- Opposite signs give an interior edge point; one zero gives an endpoint;
  two zeros give the entire edge. No zero denominator is used as a finite
  intersection.
- Deduplication and face-incidence boundary ordering agree with the
  frozen removal of redundant collinear points. They do not create extra
  sides at a vertex or hide contained edges.
- Noncollinear points in the solid give positive section area; rank-1
  or rank-0 input is represented by a family, not a default normal.
- The tetrahedral six-edge and parallelepiped twelve-edge cases remain
  separate. The affine unit-coordinate frame does not assert a unit
  Euclidean cube, equal metric edges or right angles.

A/P do not enumerate every illustrative witness already present in S for
134/145/147, but give a finite explicit construction for every parameter
assignment and all relevant rank/zero branches. The original construction
requests are therefore covered. Requiring duplicate optional witnesses
would not be a real defect in the disclosed author work.
