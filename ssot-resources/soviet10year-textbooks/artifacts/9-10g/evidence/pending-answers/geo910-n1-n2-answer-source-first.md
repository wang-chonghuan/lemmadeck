   plane and distance from one point to another. The retained6-8g unit2
   explicitly lists these four even in the preceding geometry course.
   Set is also an undefined general mathematical notion, not a replacement
   for one of the four geometric ones.

   parallel construction is the adopted parallel axiom; translation
   preserves distance as a theorem; three noncollinear points determine a
   unique circumference by theorem; equality of congruent-polygon areas is

   Drawings need concrete configurations, not a stock interpretation rule;

5. Necessary; iff; necessary only; iff. In item3 no different-plane
   hypothesis is given. Coincident planes have common points but their

6. (1) Not a universal claim: the two named points can coincide, which does
   not determine one unique line. Distinctness suffices, including two
   different points already on the given line.
   (2) If the actual H is strictly inside a nondegenerate triangle, then
   H differs from A and AH is unique. For arbitrary triangles the assertion
   and the requested inside construction are not universally valid:
   a right angle at A gives H=A, and an obtuse triangle has H outside.
   Record both readings; do not invent an acute-triangle premise.
7. Exactly1 vertex: possible, take a plane through one vertex transverse
   to the rectangle's plane. Exactly2: possible, intersect its plane in
   an edge/diagonal line. Exactly3: impossible, any three rectangle vertices
   are noncollinear and determine its whole plane, including the fourth.
8. (1) Yes: the incenter is strictly inside a nondegenerate triangle and
   is not on AB, so A/B/I determine the triangle's plane.
   (2) Not necessarily: for a triangle right-angled at C, circumcenter O
   is the midpoint of AB. Another plane through AB contains A/B/O but not
   C. If A/B/O are noncollinear, the conclusion does follow. Preserve the
   distinction instead of saying all circle centers have the same effect.
9. The ideal geometry model uses three noncollinear foot contacts:
   they determine one support plane. A fourth foot can fail to lie in it,
   so a four-leg rigid table can rock. This is not a proof that every real
   three-legged table is stable on every terrain, with every center of mass,
   friction or leg configuration. State the ideal-model scope explicitly.
10. Two distinct circle/arc points determine a line but not its plane:
    a different plane through their chord need not contain other arc points.
    Three distinct points on a nondegenerate circle are noncollinear, so
    they determine its plane and every arc point belongs to it.
11. Place the tested edge against the flat reference plane; repeat at
    different positions and turn it over, checking for gaps. A straight
    edge through two contacts in a plane lies in that plane by axiom3;
    a bowed edge can reveal gaps. A single no-gap placement alone is not
    a universal measurement proof of straightness.
12. No. Two distinct common lines yield three noncollinear common points;
    the unique-plane axiom forces the two planes to coincide, contradicting
    their given distinction. In parallel common-line case choose two
    points on one and a point on the other; in intersecting case choose
    the intersection and one other point from each.
13. A/B lie on c and differ, so c is AB. The singleton intersections
    imply pairwise distinct a/b/c and C not on c; hence A/B/C are
    noncollinear. Their plane contains a=BC and b=AC, then A1/B1,
    then the defined line A1B1, then C1.
    The notation `(A1B1)` requires two different points for a unique line;
    their distinction is not separately printed. If A1=B1=C, the line
    notation is undefined. Explain that notation-domain limitation, rather

14. A lies in both alpha and beta because A lies on a and b and those
    lines are contained in the respective planes. Therefore A is in their

15. Triangle inequality gives `AB+BC>=AC`. Equality occurs exactly when
    B lies between A and C (allowing coincident endpoints in the non-strict
    general inequality), which would put A on BC. The given A notin(BC)
