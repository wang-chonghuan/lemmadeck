## Independent Mathematics: All Twelve Options

### density-meaning: Complete True Set {A, B}

- A: TRUE. For homogeneous material and V > 0, rho = m/V. Compatible
  mass/volume units give density units, e.g. kg/m^3 or g/cm^3.
- B: TRUE. For iron, m(1 m^3) = 7800 kg/m^3 * 1 m^3 = 7800 kg.
  Also 1 cm^3 = 0.000001 m^3, so
  m = 7800 * 0.000001 kg = 0.0078 kg = 7.8 g.
  Both clauses hold.
- C: FALSE. For 1 cm^3 of osmium,
  m = 22500 kg/m^3 * 0.000001 m^3 = 0.0225 kg = 22.5 g,
  not 22500 g. The proposed mass is 1000 times the required mass.
- D: FALSE. For 1 kg of osmium,
  V = m/rho = 1 kg / (22500 kg/m^3) = 1/22500 m^3,
  approximately 0.000044444444 m^3 = 44.444444 cm^3.
  It is not 22500 m^3. The proposed volume would instead have mass
  22500 * 22500 kg = 506250000 kg.

### wood-density-units: Complete True Set {A}

First solve without using any candidate pair:
rho = 12 g / 100 cm^3 = 0.12 g/cm^3.
In SI units, m = 0.012 kg and V = 0.0001 m^3, so
rho = 0.012/0.0001 kg/m^3 = 120 kg/m^3.
The required ordered pair is therefore (0.12, 120).

- A: TRUE. Both values equal the independent calculation in the specified
  order and units.
- B: FALSE. The first value 0.12 g/cm^3 is correct, but 0.00012 kg/m^3
  is not. Converting 0.12 g/cm^3 to kg/m^3 multiplies by 1000 to give
  120, not divides by 1000.
- C: FALSE. 12 g is the total mass of 100 cm^3, not the density in
  g/cm^3. A density of 12 g/cm^3 would give a sample mass of 1200 g.
  The second value 12000 kg/m^3 is consistent with that wrong density,
  but neither value describes the given sample.
- D: FALSE. 100/12 = 25/3 is V/m, with units cm^3/g, not the requested
  density m/V. Assigning it g/cm^3 and then multiplying by 1000 gives
  the displayed 25000/3 kg/m^3, but starts from the wrong ratio.
  The actual density is 0.12 g/cm^3, not 25/3 g/cm^3.

### equal-volume-equal-mass: Complete True Set {A, B}

For equal positive cube volume v m^3:
m_brass = 8500*v kg, m_marble = 2700*v kg, m_ice = 900*v kg.
Thus m_brass > m_marble > m_ice, with ratio 85:27:9.
No numerical cube volume is assumed.

For equal mass 2 kg:
V_porcelain = 2/2300 m^3 = 1/1150 m^3
             = 20000/23 cm^3, approximately 869.565217 cm^3.
V_iron = 2/7800 m^3 = 1/3900 m^3
        = 10000/39 cm^3, approximately 256.410256 cm^3.
V_porcelain/V_iron = 7800/2300 = 78/23, approximately 3.391304.
At fixed positive mass, V = m/rho is inversely proportional to rho.

- A: TRUE. Brass is the maximum-mass cube and ice the minimum-mass cube
  at the given equal volume; both extrema are correct.
- B: TRUE. Porcelain's volume is larger at the given equal 2 kg, and
  the stated inverse-density relationship is correct.
- C: FALSE. It reverses both extrema: ice is smallest, not largest,
  and brass is largest, not smallest.
- D: FALSE. Iron's volume is smaller, not larger, and the claim that
  volume is directly proportional to density at equal mass is also false.
