Source1: put a sealed partly filled balloon in the bell chamber and evacuate
air around it. Observe slack balloon swelling; trapped gas still impacts its
wall while outside pressure decreases. Initial outward imbalance moves the
membrane. Gas is not supplied by the evacuation pipe, nor removed from inside
the balloon. Unchanged molecule number does not imply unchanged internal
pressure through changing volume. Final balance includes membrane elasticity,
so it does not require equal internal/external gas pressures alone.

cannot prove the learner devised or actually performed the experiment.

balloon's approximately spherical shape supports equal pressure in different
directions at a location. Random motion and many impacts give comparable
average effect on equally sized differently oriented surfaces, not identical
individual collisions or stopped molecules. Arbitrary rigid vessel shape
is not this evidence, and mere expansion alone is not sufficient to show
isotropy. No calibrated pressure reading or source quantitative calculation.
