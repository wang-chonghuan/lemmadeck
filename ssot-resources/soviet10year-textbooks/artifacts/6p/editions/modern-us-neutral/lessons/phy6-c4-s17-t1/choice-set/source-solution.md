## Independent full solution

The apparent weight loss is 20-18.75=1.25N. By selected concept8 this equals the weight of displaced water, so V=1.25N/(g*rho_w). The crown mass is m=20N/g. Its material density is therefore m/V=rho_w*20/1.25=16000kg/m^3. This density is below rounded gold density20000, above silver density10000, and independent of the unspecified common g.

For the second request let m_Au and m_Ag be the component masses. Mass and additive volume give:

- m_Au+m_Ag=20N/g.
- m_Au/20000+m_Ag/10000=(20N/g)/16000, with each density in kg/m^3.

Equivalently, if x=m_Au/m, then 1/16000=x/20000+(1-x)/10000. Thus x=3/4, so gold and silver mass fractions are3/4 and1/4, mass ratio3:1, and m_Au=15N/g, m_Ag=5N/g. Independent substitution check: (15N/g)/20000+(5N/g)/10000=0.00125/g in consistent SI units, which is exactly the volume inferred from displaced water. The sum of masses is20N/g.
