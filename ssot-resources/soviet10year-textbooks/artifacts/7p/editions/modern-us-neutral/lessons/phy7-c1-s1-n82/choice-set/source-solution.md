# n82 four full raw-source solutions before choice authoring

## g1/1

（1）在把球举高的过程中，球的能量发生了什么样的变化？球下落时又如何？

举高时，球与地球组成的系统的重力势能增加，人通过做功把能量传给这个系统；原题没有给出举高的速度，不能仅由高度增加判定举高过程中动能怎样变化。松手下落、忽略空气阻力时，高度逐渐减小、速度增大，所以重力势能减小、动能增加，重力势能转化为动能。若以铅板高度为势能零点，相对铅板描述球的运动，最终静止在板上时，球相应的重力势能和整体动能都为零；这只是所选参考系和零点下的机械能描述，不表示能量被消灭或内能为零。

- Separate lifting from the subsequent fall; use height and speed as different determining quantities.
- Apply work/energy reasoning to the lift: height rises, ball-Earth gravitational potential rises; no unsupported lift-speed claim.
- For free fall before contact and negligible drag, height falls while speed rises, so potential decreases and kinetic increases.
- Keep the zero-height and rest conventions explicit rather than identifying zero mechanical energy with zero total/internal energy.

Conditions: Consistent plate/Earth frame and gravitational zero. Negligible air resistance for ideal falling conversion. No prescribed lift speed or numerical givens.

Notes: Both original subrequirements answered. Mechanical energy is reference dependent; collision is treated separately. No derived numeric quantity or performed observation.

## g1/2

（2）由于铅球和铅板碰撞的结果，它们的状态发生了什么变化？

铅球碰撞后稍微变扁，铅板出现小凹痕，说明球和板都发生了形变。原书还描述立即测量可发现它们都变热了。因此，两者都发生了状态变化：形变和升温。不能仅说球变形而把板当成毫无变化，也不能把肉眼看不到温度数值说成没有升温；具体形变量和温度变化数值均未给出。

- Identify both objects named by the prompt.
- Retain the ball flattening and plate dent as the two source-prose deformation descriptions.
- Retain the warming of both as a described experimental result, not a newly performed measurement.
- Distinguish qualitative state change from absent numerical temperature/deformation data.

Conditions: Use the collision described in this lesson, not a universal result for every arbitrary collision. Measurements mentioned by source are historical teaching descriptions only.

Notes: The small raster images do not quantify dent, flattening or warming. Do not omit the plate's changes. No claim to have performed the experiment.

## g1/3

（3）当铅球与铅板发生碰撞时，铅球的机械能转变为什么能量了？

在教材所讨论的碰撞中，铅球原有机械能转化为与球和板内部状态变化有关的内能，而不是凭空消失。两者升温对应内部粒子无规则运动平均动能的变化；两者形变改变粒子的相对位置，涉及粒子间相互作用势能的变化。教材着重解释这两个内部变化途径，不能因此断言机械能全部只转化为球一方的内能，也不能排除声音或向周围传出的能量。球最终停止整体运动不表示内部粒子停止运动。

- Reject disappearance of energy when the ball stops.
- Use the warming of both objects to connect to random microscopic kinetic energy.
- Use deformation of both objects to connect to particle-relative positions and interaction potential energy.
- Name internal energy as the relevant form and preserve ball-plus-plate scope.
- Do not turn the lesson's emphasis into an exclusive quantitative energy budget.

Conditions: Source-specific collision and the textbook particle model. No measured complete energy balance is supplied. Macroscopic rest is distinct from cessation of random particle motion.

Notes: Includes explanation, not only the word 内能. Both microscopic kinetic and interaction potential terms are retained. Other energy destinations are not denied; no invented fractions or values.

## g1/4

（4）什么样的能量叫做物体的内能？

物体的内能是组成物体的所有粒子因无规则运动和彼此相互作用而具有的能量总和：包括粒子无规则运动的动能和粒子间相互作用的势能。它不同于物体作为整体运动的动能，也不同于物体与地球等外部物体之间相互作用的势能。物体整体静止不表示没有内能；仅改变观察参考系或整体高度，并不能在内部状态不变的前提下把整体机械能变化认作内能变化。

- Identify all particles composing the object, not a single molecule.
- Separate random microscopic motion from bulk translation.
- Retain interaction potential energy as well as microscopic kinetic energy.
- Exclude the body's external bulk kinetic/gravitational energy from this textbook internal-energy definition.

Conditions: Use this lesson's particle-energy model. Any comparison holding internal state fixed does not imply that real lifting or work can never change internal energy.

Notes: Full definition retained. No prohibition on work converting mechanical energy into internal energy. Internal state, reference frame and external position are different concepts.
