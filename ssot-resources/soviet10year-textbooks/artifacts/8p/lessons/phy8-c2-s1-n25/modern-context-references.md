## Kilogram

  https://www.bipm.org/en/mass-metrology/ipk
  The prototype was sanctioned in1889 and defined the SI mass unit until
  the revised definition took effect on20 May2019.
- BIPM, SI base unit: kilogram:
  https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/kilogram
  The current definition fixes the numerical value of the Planck constant,
  together with the existing definitions of the metre and second.

## Relativistic Mass

- L.B. Okun, *The Concept of Mass in the Einstein Year*, submitted3 February2006,
  arXiv:hep-ph/0602037v1, sections3 and5:
  https://arxiv.org/html/hep-ph/0602037v1
  The paper distinguishes Lorentz-invariant mass from the older
  velocity-dependent relativistic-mass terminology.

Edition treatment: keep the printed relation between m,m0,v,c exactly,
identify m in that relation as the legacy relativistic mass and m0 as the
invariant/rest mass of the same unchanged body, and explain that uniform
motion does not itself change invariant mass. Keep the source's speed values
and low-speed scope. The numerical importance of relativistic corrections
depends on required precision, so the printed30000km/s statement is not a
universal cutoff. This contextual clarification is not permission to edit a
formula or register an unproven printing erratum.

The source's additive-mass experiment is the ordinary low-speed approximation
for joined cylinders, not a universal claim about relativistic system mass.
