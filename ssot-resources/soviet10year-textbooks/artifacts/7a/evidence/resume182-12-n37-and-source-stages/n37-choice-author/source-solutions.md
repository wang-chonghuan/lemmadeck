## 798
Task: prove the expression lies strictly between 2 and 3 for every x>5.
Let E=((x+5)/(x^2-5x)-x/(x^2-25))*(x^2-25)/5.
Since x>5, x, x-5 and x+5 are nonzero; factor the denominators.
E=(x+5)^2/(5x)-x/5=(10x+25)/(5x)=2+5/x.
For x>5, 0<5/x<1, so 2<E<3. Bounds 2 and 3 are not attained.
Distinction: legal denominator cancellation and a strict reciprocal bound;
a single numerical example is not a universal proof.
