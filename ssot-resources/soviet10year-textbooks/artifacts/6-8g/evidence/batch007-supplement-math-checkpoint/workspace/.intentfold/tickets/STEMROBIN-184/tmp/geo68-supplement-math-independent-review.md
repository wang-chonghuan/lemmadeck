## Additional Author Formula Check

Task 179 contains closed-form existence intervals beyond StageA's written
four-locus algorithm. They were compared to that frozen algorithm rather
than used to replace or redo the independent derivation:

- Externally disjoint originals: every auxiliary radius is at most ri+r,
  so the frozen center-distance upper bound requires
  `d<=r1+r2+2r`. The E1/E2 difference is still abs(r1-r2)<d.
  Therefore the author's `r>=(d-r1-r2)/2` existence threshold follows
  from the same necessary-and-sufficient auxiliary-circle conditions.
  The equality case is one center, not two.
- Strictly nested originals, `0<=d<R-q`: for target external to the
  smaller and internal to the larger, the frozen pair radii are q+r,
  R-r. Their difference bound is `abs(2r-(R-q))<=d`, giving the
  author's first interval. For a target enclosing the smaller while
  internal to the larger, they are r-q,R-r, and the difference bound
  is `abs(2r-(R+q))<=d`, giving the second interval.
  The first interval lies strictly within 0<r<R; the second within
  q<r<R because d<R-q. Thus no negative/zero auxiliary radius is
  improperly admitted. Their sum bounds are R+q and R-q, both >d.
  Remaining sign/type possibilities have auxiliary differences >d and
  cannot add an omitted nested solution.
- At d=0 the intervals reduce to the two positive radii
  (R-q)/2 and (R+q)/2. Equal positive auxiliary circumferences then
  give the full center circumferences of radii (R+q)/2 and (R-q)/2,
  respectively, exactly the frozen no-versus-infinite intersection rule.
  The coordinate formula with denominator 2d is used only for d>0.
- For externally tangent or crossing originals, the E1/E2 pair has
  strict difference/sum bounds and gives two centers for every r>0.
  For internally tangent originals it has the positive difference
  equality and gives one. This matches StageA; other pairings remain
  available and may increase the total.
