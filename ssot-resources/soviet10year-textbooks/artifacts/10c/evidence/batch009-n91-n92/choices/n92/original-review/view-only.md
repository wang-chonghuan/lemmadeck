1. **q16: direct algebraic reversal of the unproved target.** No product-to-sum
   derivation is necessary to reconstruct the candidate expression from the
   displayed equality. Remove the target/cancellation cue from the selection
   surface, or make reverse consistency insufficient and require judgment of

2. **q17: coefficient matching substitutes for proof.** Denote the formal
   candidate identities by P=a(1+b cos4x) and sinx cos4x=c(sin5x+d sin3x).
   Distributing the supplied bridge yields
   a sinx+abc sin5x+abcd sin3x. Matching the printed target specifies
   a=1/8, bc=-1/2, d=-1, without verifying either identity. Recognition of a
   valid proof could be acceptable; uniquely selecting identities by this
   unproved target alone is the leak.
3. **q18: target reconstruction of intermediate signs and scale.** Write the
   candidate forms as sin5x cos3x=a(sin8x+b sin2x) and
   sin2x cos6x=(sin8x+c sin4x)/2. Using the unlisted common branch

   independently tested. The view therefore does not safeguard the full
