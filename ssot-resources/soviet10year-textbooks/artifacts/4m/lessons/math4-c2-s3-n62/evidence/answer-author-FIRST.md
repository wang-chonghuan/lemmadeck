## Rule And Prose

For an exact decimal A/10^k, multiplying by 10^n gives A*10^n/10^k;
multiplying by 10^-n gives A/10^(k+n). The first shifts the decimal point
right n places, the second left n places; padding zeros does not change A.
The retained examples are 4.576*10=45.76, 4.576*100=457.6,
0.124124*10000=1241.24, 3.7*100=370, 26.4*0.1=2.64,
and 003.7*0.01=0.037. Leading zeros in 003.7 are harmless.
The source p0233#9 statement of one rightward place is false for *100;
its registered correction is two places, not an altered numerical example.
