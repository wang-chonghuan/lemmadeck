FULL ENGLISH EDITION TEXT:

# 44. Sound Resonance

A sounding object can vibrate freely.
A periodic external force can also drive its motion.
Near its natural frequency, the driven response can grow greatly: this is resonance.

Two identical tuning forks demonstrate **sound resonance**.
Set them a short distance apart on wooden resonance boxes.
Let the box openings face each other (Figure 83).

Tap one arm of one fork with a small hammer.
The other fork soon begins to sound as well.
Sound waves from the first apply a periodic force to it.

Their matching natural frequencies allow a large response.
Different natural frequencies give a much weaker response.
The other fork then usually makes no clearly audible sound.

Attach a light metal clip to either identical fork.
The clip changes that fork's natural frequency.
The original resonance weakens or is no longer audible.

Figure 83

Tuning forks have little damping, so their resonance curve is sharp.
This is the behavior discussed in Section 14.


FULL CHINESE EDITION TEXT (context only, DO NOT RENDER ITS TEXT):

# 44. 声的共鸣

发声物体可以做自由振动。
在周期性外力作用下，它也可以做受迫振动。
外力频率接近物体的固有频率时，受迫振动可以显著增强，这就是共振。

两只相同的音叉可以用来演示**声的共振**。
把它们放在相隔不远的木制共鸣箱上。
让两个木箱的开口彼此相对（图83）。

用小锤轻敲一只音叉的一个臂。
不久，另一只音叉也开始发声。
第一只音叉发出的声波，对另一只施加周期性的力。

两只音叉的固有频率相同，所以另一只可以产生较大的振动响应。
如果两只音叉的固有频率不同，响应就会弱得多。
这时，另一只通常不会发出明显的声音。

在原来相同的任一音叉上固定一个轻金属夹。
金属夹会改变这只音叉的固有频率。
原来的共振因而明显减弱，或不再产生可听到的响应。

图83

音叉的振动阻尼较小，所以共振曲线比较尖锐。
这与第14节的讨论一致。
