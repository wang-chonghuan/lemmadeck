The four `source-028*.md` files remain untouched after the respective candidate was read.
These notes expose limitations of the initial human-readable freeze; they do not claim
another pixel viewing or silently replace its independent first reading.

1. In `source-0282.md`, the count annotation saying 24 body lines / 26 total is an
   arithmetic error. The frozen lines actually total 23 body lines + heading + footer = 25.
   The candidate's 25-line native audit is not a line-count defect.

   for the old print's baseline solid-dot stops and parentheses. They must not be used
   to allege that all ASCII stops/parentheses in the candidate are wrong.
   The visually differentiated semicolon after `波` remains a specific punctuation finding.

   viewed 0283 grid begins `续光谱`; this supports the candidate's `发射连` + `续光谱`.
   No confirmed tail-character error is charged to the candidate. A repeat image viewing
   was deliberately not performed; this original single-view character reading is tentative.

   were not confidently resolved. Only the clearly frozen `天蓝色` / three dots is fully

5. 0283 heavier typography is real, particularly `最大` and `看不见`; full phrase-boundary

   comma-versus-enumeration-comma shape is not sufficiently certain from this one view.
