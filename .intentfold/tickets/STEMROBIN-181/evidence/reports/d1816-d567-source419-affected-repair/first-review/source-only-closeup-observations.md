## Image 2: caption

Source-coordinate crop, xyxy: `[180, 1880, 1450, 2040]`.

- Exactly two complete, separate physical text baselines.
- Literal glyph reading, retaining solid dots as `．` rather than empty circles:
  `图 V． 发射光谱：1—连续的；2—钠的；3—氢的；4—氦的．`
  `吸收光谱：5—太阳的；6—钠的；7—氢的；8—氦的．`
- The marks after V and at the ends of both caption rows are solid dot-shaped
  periods. A raster scan cannot distinguish ASCII `.` from fullwidth `．`
  codepoints, but it does distinguish these solid dots from hollow `。`.
- The item separators are semicolons; the heading separators are colons;
  item number-to-description connectors are long horizontal dashes.
- The element names are 钠, 氢, 氦, not similarly shaped substitutes.
- There is no third caption row in this source-only caption crop.
