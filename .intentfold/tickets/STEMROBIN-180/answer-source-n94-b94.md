## Definitions And Assumptions

`p0247#2`: q is the heat released when 1 kg of a fuel burns completely.
`p0247#3`: units are J/kg, kcal/kg, cal/g; kcal/kg and cal/g are numerically
equivalent. These are energy/mass, not specific-heat-capacity units.
`p0247#4`-`#5`: values are experimentally measured.
`p0247#8` and `p0248#1`: the peat example explains per-kilogram released heat.
`p0248#2`: Q = q m; Q is energy, m mass, q energy per mass.

With q in J/kg use mass in kg. Complete combustion is required. Q is total
released heat, not useful heat delivered to a device; no efficiency is supplied.
For a volume, m = rho V and Q = q rho V, dimensionally
(J/kg)(kg/m^3)(m^3) = J. No task, quantity or scientific text was changed.
