## Original Tasks

1. **(1) 如何解释在熔解进行的整个过程中，晶体的温度始终保持不变？**

   晶体达到熔点后，继续吸收的能量用来破坏结晶结构，使固体逐步变成液体，
   并增加它的内能，而不是使温度升高。因此，熔化尚未完成时，温度保持在
   熔点；全部熔化后继续加热，液体的温度才会上升。

   Source: `p0260#6-8` states the plateau, the crystal-structure energy
   requirement and the internal-energy change. The inspected B-C plateau of
   original graph229 corroborates the observation. This concerns ordinary
   pure-crystal equilibrium melting at fixed pressure with both phases present,
   not every conceivable melting process.

2. **(2) 当晶体正在熔解时，加热器中燃烧着的燃料所放出的能量都用在哪儿了？**

   燃料释放并传给晶体的能量，用来破坏结晶结构，使晶体熔化，并增加它的
   内能，而不是在熔化阶段升高晶体的温度。按原课文关注的理想化情形，
   供给晶体的能量用于这种状态变化。

   Source: `p0260#6-8`. Crucially, `#8` refers to the energy the crystal
   **receives**. Neither the original prose nor the prompt gives heater
   efficiency or a complete fuel-energy allocation. No conclusion that100%
   of released fuel energy enters the crystal, invented percentage, or
   unsupported destination of the remainder is used. This answers the source's
   conceptual melting question without pretending to determine a full fuel
   budget. Negligible external work is the usual internal-energy approximation.

3. **(3) 什么是熔解热？**

   在熔点时，把1千克固态结晶物质完全变成同温度的液体所需要的热量，
   叫作这种物质的熔解热。它表示单位质量的物质熔化所需要的热量，
   用字母λ表示。

   Source: `p0260#9` defines the quantity, `p0260#12` gives lambda,
   and `p0261#1` establishes same-temperature conversion. The specimen is
   already at its melting point at the specified pressure; heat used to warm
   it up to that point is not part of this definition.

4. **(4) 熔解热都有哪些量度单位？**

   焦耳/千克、千卡/千克和卡/克，即J/kg、kcal/kg和cal/g。

   Source: `p0260#10-11` gives the complete list. Each unit is energy per
   mass. The original table header independently corroborates the units;
   no conversion constant or new numerical value is needed.
