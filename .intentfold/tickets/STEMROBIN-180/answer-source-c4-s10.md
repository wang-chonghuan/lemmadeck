## Seven Tasks

- 1: Molecules remain in continuous random thermal motion. Gravity and this
  motion produce a statistical atmosphere; molecules do not simply settle as
  motionless particles on the surface. Do not claim gravity ceases to act.
- 2: Most atmospheric molecules have speeds below the escape speed. The source
  near-Earth11.2km/s value and “绝大多数” qualification matter: the speed
  distribution has a tail and some particles can escape. Average speed below
  escape speed is not a proof that absolutely every molecule is bound.
- 3: Density decreases with altitude in the source model. The5.4km half and
  11km quarter values are approximate textbook examples, not universal exact
  density ratios for all weather, temperature and atmospheric compositions.
- g2-1: Insert pipette in liquid, seal top with a finger, then raise it.
  Liquid remains while the lower opening experiences outside atmospheric
  pressure and trapped upper air has lower pressure. Static support is the
  pressure difference balancing the liquid column, approximately
  p_outside-p_inside=ρgh, ignoring meniscus effects. Reopening top admits air,
  removes that support and liquid drains. Include procedure and observation;
  point selection cannot verify doing the experiment.
- g2-2: Squeeze bulb to expel air; immerse tip and release bulb. Its expansion
  lowers internal pressure so outside atmospheric pressure pushes liquid in.
  Squeeze the filled dropper to raise internal pressure and eject drops.
  A picture of a filled dropper does not supply a measured volume or pressure.
- g2-3: For a compressible-reservoir fountain-pen filling mechanism, compress
  to expel air, immerse the intake, then release; outside pressure drives ink
  into the lower-pressure reservoir. Do not generalize the same actuator to
  every pen design or call the mechanism an attractive suction force.
- 4: With inverted bottle mouth below trough surface, air cannot freely enter.
  Reduced trapped-air pressure plus the outside atmospheric support arrests
  outflow in hydrostatic balance. If the trough drops below the mouth, air
  enters in bubbles and water flows out; the water raises the trough surface.
  Flow stops again after the mouth is submerged and the lower trapped
  pressure restores balance. Include all three requested states and causes.

All7 are explanatory/practical source tasks and should remain ungraded written
work with complete explanations. New choices can assess inference, not certify
physical apparatus construction, operation or safety.
