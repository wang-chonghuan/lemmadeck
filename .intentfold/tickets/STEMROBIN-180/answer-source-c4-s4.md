## 1 / g1 / Definition

A liquid's free surface is the surface not in contact with the vessel walls,
normally the exposed liquid-air boundary. This is not its wall/bottom contact
surface or every boundary of a filled sealed vessel. Determinate short
definition; grading can be considered only after the final modern wording,
without imposing an arbitrary unique synonym.

## 2 / g1 / Horizontal Surface

For liquid at rest in a broad stationary vessel under gravity and nearly
uniform external pressure, a sloped free surface would allow liquid to flow
from higher to lower regions. Equilibrium leaves the surface at one height,
perpendicular to local gravity. This is the course's elementary horizontal
surface model, not an unconditional claim about accelerating/rotating vessels,
waves, capillary menisci or a free-falling vessel. Explain the actual gravity/
flow relationship, not merely repeat “liquids are horizontal.” Ungraded.

## 3 / g1 / Instrument Name

The instrument is a level (水平仪).107 illustrates the vial instrument;108
shows an independently made plumb-based alternative. Determinate naming
question, no measured reading or automatic numeric value.

## 4 / g1 / Gas Surface

Under the ordinary course conditions a gas is not a condensed body with a
stable upper free boundary like liquid. Its separated moving molecules spread
through the available vessel space, rather than maintaining a fixed volume
below a liquid-like exposed surface. The source's weak-attraction explanation
is its qualitative model; do not claim gas molecules have no interactions or
that every mixture, gravitational atmosphere or temperature is uniform.
No sharp container-wall boundary is a liquid free surface. Ungraded explanation.

## g2-1 / Plumb Line, Set Square And Drawing

Let the liquid settle in a broad stationary vessel. Suspend a plumb line near
the surface without disturbing the liquid; wait until its bob is still. Align
one right-angle side of the set square with that vertical line. Its other
right-angle side is horizontal and should be parallel to the observed free
surface. Repeat in another nonparallel direction to check a plane rather than
only one horizontal line. Draw the vessel, liquid surface, vertical line/bob,
set square and marked right angle/parallel horizontal side.

This is a practical check and drawing: no invented personal observation,
surface height or angle. A symbolic/right-angle construction explains the
method but does not certify that the learner performed it. Ungraded.

## g2-2 / Construct And Use Paper Level

Construct the complete stable triangular frame and hanging plumb line from
thick paper/cardboard as108 shows. Provide a straight supporting base and a
reference direction perpendicular to that base. A freely hanging settled line
on that direction means the base is horizontal. Establish/check the reference
using a known horizontal surface or reversal calibration; do not invent a
printed reference label or dimension in108.

Use it on the windowsill, tabletop and floor as requested, in at least two
nonparallel directions. Wait for oscillations to stop and record actual
observations; identify any slope rather than prefill that all three are
horizontal. Keep the frame flat/stable, the line free and the bob clear of
supports. Construction, calibration and measurements are ungraded.

## g2-3 / Level Board And Ball

Place the board on supports and adjust small wooden wedges while observing
the level. Check two nonparallel directions and recheck after each adjustment
until both are level. Put the ball down gently, initially at rest, on a smooth
stable region. In the elementary horizontal-plane model gravity has no
component along the board and the ball should not begin rolling from rest.

Do not reverse the implication into proof that every stationary ball certifies
a horizontal board: surface roughness, dents or static resistance can hold it
on a small slope. The level is the actual orientation check. Do not claim
the perspective drawing measures slope or fabricate a performed experiment.
Retain board, wedges, level and ball/observation task. Ungraded.
