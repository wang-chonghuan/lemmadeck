## Task 1

Original: `1. 物体的总机械能是什么？`

Mechanical energy is the sum of kinetic and potential energy, `E=K+U`.
For a body moving near Earth, the shorthand assigns system gravitational
potential energy to the body after fixing the Earth reference and zero level.
In the uniform-gravity point-mass model, `K=mv^2/2`, `U=mgz`, and therefore
`E=mv^2/2+mgz`. Potential energy belongs to the interacting system; internal
energy is not included merely by calling this total mechanical energy.
This definition question needs a complete explanation, not a numeric key.

## Task 2

Original: `2. 当物体在重力作用下运动时，物体的总能量守恒定律的内容是什么？`

For the body-Earth mechanical system with gravity the only force doing work
and no dissipation or other energy transfer, `K+U_g` is constant. Thus
`K_1+U_{g,1}=K_2+U_{g,2}` and `Delta K=-Delta U_g`.
In approximately uniform gravity, this is
`mv_1^2/2+mgz_1=mv_2^2/2+mgz_2`.
Downward motion can convert gravitational potential energy into kinetic
energy; upward motion can make the opposite conversion. Conservation is
about the sum, not about either term remaining separately constant.

The original does not clearly distinguish all energy from mechanical energy
or exclude other forces doing work. Mechanical conservation does not follow
merely from the existence of gravity. With drag, energy can enter internal
energy while total energy of a suitably closed larger system is conserved.
Modern clarification may state the ideal conditions without changing RAW.

## Task 3

Original: `3. 物体从地球上某高度降落. 在落地时它的速度等于30米/秒，物体是从多高降落的？`

The original specifies the final speed but not the initial speed, drag, or
a numerical gravity convention. It does not uniquely fix a numerical height.
For gravity-only fall with approximately uniform gravitational acceleration
and initial speed `v_i`, the energy equation gives
`h=(v_f^2-v_i^2)/(2g)`. With rest release, `v_i=0`, `v_f=30 m/s`, hence
`h=(30 m/s)^2/(2g)`.

If explicitly using `g=9.8 m/s^2`, this is about `45.9 m`;
if explicitly using `g=10 m/s^2`, it is `45 m`. Neither convention is a
printed extra given. If drag does work `W_drag`, the more general equation is
`h=(v_f^2-v_i^2)/(2g)-W_drag/(mg)`; the final speed alone cannot determine
height without the other terms. No unconditional original numeric key is
claimed. The modern prompt may explicitly state rest release and the ideal
gravity-only model, while retaining the given final speed and source snapshot.
